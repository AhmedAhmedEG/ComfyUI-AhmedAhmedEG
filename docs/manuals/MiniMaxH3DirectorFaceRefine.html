Detection, tracked crop generation and stitching

FaceRefine is a decoded-video stage. The Master first produces RGB frames, then the stage detects/tracks a selected face, creates a temporally consistent crop sequence, constructs native H3 conditioning, samples the crop latent and stitches the refined frames back into the original canvas. It does not replace the whole shot with a portrait image or turn a face detector into a subject-identity model.

Connect face_refine → Master.face_refine. enabled bypasses the stage when false. The detector needs compatible YOLO weights, normally under models/ultralytics/bbox; detector names that file. Missing dependencies/weights and a scene with no detectable face are different situations. When tracking finds no usable crops, the adapter can return the original frames with a note.

Choosing and tracking the crop

confidence is the detection-score threshold. Raising it rejects uncertain detections but can miss small/profile faces. select=largest_face chooses by face size; centre_most chooses proximity to the frame centre. In a two-person scene these criteria can select a different person than you intended. They are not names supplied by Reference Pack.

crop_factor expands the crop around the detected face to provide context. More surrounding head/shoulders can help continuity but dilutes the face's share of the crop. crop_size supplies the manual square sampling canvas. canvas_mode=manual uses that requested canvas; auto_capped_768 delegates adaptive size selection to the crop engine, capped by its supported canvas policy. These are crop dimensions, not final movie dimensions.

What changes the reconstructed face

prompt is the crop-stage description. strength maps to crop denoise; high strengths allow larger changes and can alter identity/expression. steps, sampler and scheduler belong to this extra sample, not the first-pass Settings. Optional sigmas supplies a crop schedule. seed_mode=inherit uses the shot seed; offset uses seed + 1 modulo the 64-bit range. This improves repeatability, not guaranteed identity preservation.

The crop sequence is aligned to H3's temporal grid, padding a short sequence with its last crop where needed. Only relevant decoded frames are stitched into the shot. The stage uses the compatible shot model and audiovisual VAEs; it does not require you to connect a separate image-diffusion pipeline to this configuration node.

Masks, color and blending

paste_region selects face_only, face_ellipse or full_crop. A narrow region confines replacement; full_crop permits larger changes around hair/background. mask_dilation expands the replacement mask and feather softens its edge. Too little feather makes a hard boundary; too much can blur nearby details or dilute the refinement. Both are crop/mask controls, not global video blur.

colour_match controls matching between refined crop and original color statistics. blend controls the strength of the stitched result. A zero blend can leave the result visually unchanged while still paying generation cost. In a chained video, seam fades can reduce an abrupt onset/exit of the crop stage. Changing color-match or blend can make a useful refinement less visible without changing the crop sample itself.

A careful first test

Use a short single-person shot with a visible face. Keep the default detector/confidence, manual 512 crop, strength 0.35 and one crop-stage schedule. Compare face detail, expression, hair boundary and frame-to-frame motion against the unrefined shot. If the wrong person is enhanced, change selection/crop strategy rather than increasing strength. If a face flashes at the boundary, inspect tracking and stitch masks. FaceRefine cannot restore truthful detail that the original model never represented.
