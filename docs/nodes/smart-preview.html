Smart Preview

Review the latest completed clip or the current full sequence in the browser.

Use it when: Watch results update after each completed clip without exporting the entire movie after every run.

1. What it does

Queuing alone preserves the old preview. Once a predecessor is successfully replaced, its continuity-dependent successors lose their old cached files and approvals. Regenerated successors appear as they finish; independent clips remain.

2. Connect it

Master.project_state → Smart Preview.project_state

3. First use

  1. Connect the project_state output from the Master you want to inspect.
  2. Choose Latest clip to review one result, or Full video to inspect the sequence and its joins.
  3. Use the stage selector for available First Pass or Refine stages; keep Final Output for the normal result.
  4. Choose preview resolution, autoplay and Save preview as needed. These do not change generation settings.

6. What comes out

This output/display node has no output sockets; its connection schedules upstream work.

7. Limits & troubleshooting

  • The preview reads current server-side results. Approval is a Master reuse decision, not a requirement for viewing a completed result.
  • Saved Take history is removed. Regeneration replaces the current result and evicts superseded preview revisions.
  • Original Resolution preserves generation dimensions; 540p/720p/1080p are playback caps, not model settings.
  • Browser cache cannot restore deleted server latents, and a preview MP4 is not a continuation checkpoint.
  • Starting All clips preserves the old preview until a successful replacement. Replacing a predecessor deletes its continuity-dependent successors’ cached results; independent/source clips stop that chain. Both scopes prebuffer completed revisions with autoplay off.
  • Playback can start while clips are still downloading. Full Video reuses the same per-clip video fragments as Latest Clip, with one joined soundtrack to preserve audio fades and gain matching. Save preview remains a separate downloadable MP4. Browsers without supported MediaSource playback fall back to ordinary MP4 streaming.
  • Continuity prefixes and visible PCM are resampled together before the join, avoiding separate resampler edges at the cut. This helps seam artifacts, not poor audio generated within a clip.
  • Save preview explicitly downloads the displayed scope and revision through ComfyUI authentication, with progress and error feedback. A scope/revision change while downloading does not change which result that download saves.

8. Check your setup

Confirm the model family, source media and setting scope before comparing output. A visible control is not proof that a missing provider or model actually ran.
